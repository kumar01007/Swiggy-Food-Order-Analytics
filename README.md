
# Swiggy Food Order Analytics

A SQL-based business analytics project focused on understanding food ordering patterns, restaurant performance, customer satisfaction, pricing, and revenue trends using Swiggy order data.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze food delivery order data and generate business insights that can help understand customer behavior, identify high-performing restaurants and categories, and evaluate revenue and pricing patterns.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- SQL (PostgreSQL)
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Analysis
- Git & GitHub

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

The project includes analysis of:

- Total orders, revenue, and average order value
- Top restaurants by revenue and order volume
- Most frequently ordered dishes
- Monthly and weekly ordering trends
- Revenue performance across cities
- Revenue contribution by food category
- Veg vs Non-Veg order distribution
- Customer rating distribution and satisfaction
- Top-rated and lowest-rated restaurants
- Pricing patterns across cities and categories
- High-value orders and frequently repeated dishes
- Relationship between food price and customer ratings
- Revenue concentration across restaurants

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis can be used to identify:

- High-performing restaurants and food categories
- Cities with stronger revenue performance
- Customer preferences across food types and price ranges
- Categories contributing significantly to overall revenue
- Pricing patterns associated with customer ratings
- Restaurants and products that may require further attention

These insights can support decisions related to restaurant partnerships, pricing, promotions, and category-level strategy.
